## What it is

HR Command Center is a workspace for an HR consultant and the authorized staff of the organizations they support, across multiple clients and locations. It brings together:

- requests from HR, office managers and employees;
- verified employee identity and site assignments;
- case analysis, tasks and approvals;
- controlled documents and reviewed communications;
- compliance follow-up and reporting.

## A deliberate boundary

HR and the client keep decision authority. The system makes required evidence, next actions and unresolved exceptions visible. It does not present an unverified calculation or status as a final employment decision.

## Who uses it

Access is scoped by role. An employee sees only their own requests. An office manager sees only the cases for their assigned program. HR specialists work client-wide, and client administrators manage accounts, configuration and records controls.

## What's in it

The build covers a command center of cases and tasks, employee records, leave and accommodations, employee relations, onboarding, separations, credential tracking, templates, approvals and document packets, and analytics.

Every module carries an explicit status label: implemented, verified against fictional fixtures, prepared, or pending. None is described as production-complete.

## Where it stands

This is a demonstration build that runs on fictional data only. Live email, payroll, e-signature and mailbox connections are disabled, and no real employee data belongs in it.
